## Liminal Horror

The mysteries *Mannequin Season*, *Silver Threads*, *Low Signal*, *Sweetwater* and *The County Fair* (`src/hunt/mysteries/`) use creatures, factions and concepts adapted from the Liminal Horror SRD v3.0 (https://liminalhorrorrpg.com) by Goblin Archives LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): Puppets, Goloch, the Bureau, Shades, Panopticon, Frog-Men, Dead Gods, Gremlins, resonant artifacts, and the Doom Clock structure. They have been adapted to this project's Monster Hunt rules; the stories and all rules text are original. Liminal Horror is copyright by Goblin Archives LLC.
